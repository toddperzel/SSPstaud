01 // FIT PREDICTOR

Her size for this style, before she asks

STAUD Catalog Challenge

A "Medium" is a concept, not a measurement

STAUD publishes 1 body chart across 540 products. It states a Medium is 36.5–38" bust. However, the product table for the Evie Top measures a Medium at 30.5", while the Sorrento Sweater measures at 55.5".

Research Insight: In a study of 450 female online shoppers, 64.7% did not know their bust measurement, and 79.8% did not know their hip measurement.
The Solution

One common scale for every size & style

Fit Predictor places every size of every style on a unified scale built from real purchase and return behaviors. It matches the shopper to the item instantly without requiring body tape measurements.

Shopper Tailored: Returning shoppers get instant recommendations (0 clicks). Mobile/Hurried shoppers get a 1-click prompt.
02 // SIZE RECOVERY

Recovering demand when her size is unavailable

STAUD Catalog Challenge

The dress is in stock, but her size is gone

When a shopper's suggested size is sold out on a product page, the item is effectively out of stock for her, offering no direct path to in-stock alternatives.

STAUD Audit Finding: 218 out of 585 women's clothing items reviewed on staud.clothing had at least one size unavailable (>37% of catalog).
The Solution

1 Click to 5 styles available in her size

When her size is unavailable, Size Recovery dynamically embeds a "View Similar Styles" trigger that presents 5 curated alternatives immediately available in her exact size.

Zero Friction: The shopper never has to restart her fit discovery or re-filter catalog lists.
03 // DYNAMIC SIZE GUIDES

Eliminating the ease gap between body and garment

STAUD Catalog Challenge

5 confusing tables in 1 pop-up

Opening the standard STAUD size guide presents body charts, international conversions, shoes, and swimwear guidance simultaneously—making a simple sizing question difficult to answer.

The Solution

Product-specific dynamic charts

Dynamic Size Guides merge body measurements with intended garment ease for that specific item. If the Kent Tank runs large, its body measurements adjust dynamically while highlighting her recommended column.

04 & 05 // VISUAL RELEVANCE & MOTION

Virtual Try-On and Catalog Drape

Virtual Try-On

The garment on a body like hers

Renders STAUD garments across representative body shapes using imagery STAUD already publishes. No customer photo uploads or expensive multi-model studio shoots required.

Drape Motion
